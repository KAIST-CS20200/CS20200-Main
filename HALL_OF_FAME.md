# Hall of Fame

This page is dedicated to showcasing the best term projects from each semester.
These projects have been selected based on their creativity, technical
excellence, and overall impact. We hope that these projects will inspire current
and future students to strive for excellence in their own work.

## Spring 2026

- [Ouroboros](https://github.com/T9Y9/Ouroboros/tree/main) by Hyeongseop Kim (20240217)

This game was selected as the Best Game Award winner for its creative and original concept, strong requirements analysis and design, as well as clear and concise code and documentation.
